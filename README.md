🚗 Watch Your Head

«A fast-paced 3D game jam project built in three days, featuring dynamic difficulty, chaotic traffic, and a distinctive visual style.»

---

📌 Overview

Watch Your Head is a 3D game created during a 3-day game jam.

The project was designed around a simple but chaotic gameplay concept: survive as long as possible while driving through a constantly changing road filled with randomly generated obstacles.

The game features a distinctive visual style developed specifically for the project, rather than directly following the visual direction of other games.

---

🎮 Gameplay & Core Loop

* Three Vehicle Classes: Choose between three different vehicle classes with unique characteristics.
* Class A — Small Car: A compact and agile vehicle designed for quick movement and easier maneuvering.
* Class B — Heavy Truck: A large truck with different movement characteristics and a larger physical presence on the road.
* Four-Lane Road: The player drives across a road consisting of four lanes.
* Random Obstacles: Four different types of obstacles can randomly appear on the road, forcing the player to react quickly.
* Time-Based Gameplay: The objective is to survive and keep driving for as long as possible while the difficulty increases over time.
* Dynamic Difficulty: The game progressively becomes more challenging as the player continues playing.

---

🎭 Key Features

* Dynamic Difficulty: Gameplay intensity changes as the player survives longer.
* Randomized Obstacles: Road hazards appear unpredictably, keeping each run different.
* Vehicle Classes: Different vehicle types provide different movement and handling characteristics.
* Fast-Paced Gameplay: The player must constantly react to obstacles while maintaining control.
* 3D Environment: The entire experience is presented in a stylized 3D environment.

---

🎨 Creative Vision & Concept

Watch Your Head was created as a rapid game jam experiment focused on turning a simple driving concept into a chaotic and replayable experience.

A major part of the project was developing its own distinctive visual identity instead of directly copying the style of existing projects.

The short three-day development period also required focusing on the core gameplay loop, quick iteration, and making the most important mechanics work within a limited amount of time.
